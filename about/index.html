01 / Our story

Production is complex.
Getting answers
shouldn’t be.

An alert tells you something is wrong. It rarely tells you what changed, who should respond, or which evidence explains the failure. That context is scattered across logs, deploys, code, and conversations.

OpsTracer brings those pieces together. We connect on-call routing and incident investigation in one workflow, so the path from the first page to the root cause stays traceable.

Built by a small engineering team at c0x12c Inc., OpsTracer is shaped around the work of engineers under pressure: finding the right responder, understanding customer impact, and leaving behind a prevention plan.

Less guesswork. More understanding.

02 / What we believe

Trust is built
on evidence.

The principles behind every alert we route,
every investigation, and every answer.

  1. 01

    Show your work.

    An AI answer should lead you back to the logs, deploys, and code behind it. Every conclusion needs a trail your team can follow.

  2. 02

    Respect the responder.

    Under pressure, clarity matters. Bring the right engineer the context they need, without adding another place to search.

  3. 03

    Leave the system better.

    Resolving an incident is only part of the job. Turn what happened into an RCA and a prevention plan the next responder can use.
