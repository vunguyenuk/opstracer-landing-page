OpsTracer does not ask engineers to trust a black box during a production incident. It builds a traceable case
file: what happened, when it happened, which systems confirm it, and how strongly the evidence supports
the conclusion.

  1. Alert

    Respond to the right signal,
    not every signal.

  2. On-call

    Reach the engineer who can fix it,
    not just the next name in rotation.

  3. Investigation

    Follow one trace instead of
    jumping between six tools.

  4. Root cause

    Prove what caused the failure,
    not just what happened nearby.

  5. Prevention

    Fix the system instead of
    writing another incident report.

The RCA is not
paperwork. It is
the investigation
artifact.

Because OpsTracer captures the timeline and evidence while the incident is unfolding, the postmortem starts complete. Engineers spend less time reconstructing events and more time deciding how to prevent the next failure.
