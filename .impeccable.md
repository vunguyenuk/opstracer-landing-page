## Design Context

### Users
OpsTracer serves on-call engineers, SRE and platform leads, and engineering leaders who need to route incidents, investigate production changes, preserve evidence, and explain root cause under pressure.

### Brand Personality
Technical, decisive, and evidence-led. The interface should feel like a precise operational instrument: dense enough to communicate capability, calm enough to inspire trust, and never decorative without meaning.

### Aesthetic Direction
Dark industrial editorial styling inspired by The Grid AI: near-black surfaces, cool white typography, restrained electric blue accents, structured pixel and line motifs, square geometry, and purposeful asymmetric compositions. Motion should reveal how raw operational noise becomes organized, traceable evidence.

### Design Principles
- Show transformation, not decoration: every visual effect should reinforce the path from alert to evidence.
- Preserve strong hierarchy and generous negative space around the primary message.
- Use the blue accent sparingly for direction, state, and action.
- Prefer crisp code-native geometry over generic illustration treatments.
- Keep motion slow enough to understand, performant, and respectful of reduced-motion preferences.
